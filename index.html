About Me

Hello! My name is Payman and I'm a Technical Product Developer specializing in Industrial IoT and SCADA systems for the energy sector. My journey in software development started back in 2005 when I began developing web applications professionally — turns out building solutions for industrial systems taught me a lot about both software architecture and the energy sector!

Fast-forward to today, and I've had the privilege of working at various companies across different sectors for nearly two decades. I'm now at E.ON, one of Europe's largest energy corporations, where my expertise in system design, programming, and object-oriented software design comes together with my deep understanding of energy systems.
